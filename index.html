Research programme

Catalan

A programme about intelligent search. It does not ask a system to solve a stated problem.

Here is a space of possibilities. Learn what is worth exploring.

Several properties can make a search intelligent in that sense. One is that the policy deciding what to try next is updated by what earlier attempts returned. Another is that the thing being searched for is an algorithm rather than a setting of parameters. The programme puts six questions and holds them open rather than assuming the answers.

The questions

  1. Adaptive search. Whether a system can change its search policy while it is still solving the problem.
  2. Compute allocation. How to distribute a fixed budget across competing hypotheses.
  3. Pruning. Whether reasoning can rule out unpromising regions before paying to evaluate them.
  4. Algorithm discovery. Whether search can discover algorithms at all.
  5. Transfer. Whether search knowledge carries across unrelated problems.
  6. Scaling. How performance changes with reasoning, evaluation and search compute.

What has come back

Log 1 · markets · in progress

A tree search over six thousand strategies spent most of its budget discovering a defect in its own scorer.

Sixteen language models wrote intraday strategies for the Nifty 50 between June and September 2026. The leaderboard it produced is void, and why it is void is the result. Twenty tree snapshots are open to explore alongside the log.
